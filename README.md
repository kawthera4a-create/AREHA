# ARIHA — Flowers & Gifts Experience

> **What do you want to say?**

ARIHA is a UI/UX design concept for a personalized flowers and gifts application that helps users choose meaningful gifts based on the occasion, relationship, emotions, and message they want to express.

Instead of starting with *“What should I buy?”*, ARIHA starts with:

**“What do you want to say?”**

## 📌 Project Overview

ARIHA is a personalized flowers and gifts application designed to make the process of choosing and sending gifts easier, more meaningful, and emotionally connected.

The application targets people who want to give flowers or gifts to someone important in their lives, whether for a special occasion or simply to express their feelings.

Choosing the right gift can often be confusing, especially when users are unsure about what to buy or how to express their emotions.

ARIHA addresses this problem by connecting **the user's feelings, occasion, relationship, and desired message** with personalized gift recommendations.

The core idea is simple:

> **Don't start by choosing a gift. Start by choosing what you want to say.**

---

## 🎯 Our Goals

- Solve the problem of choosing the right gift.
- Improve the experience of purchasing flowers and gifts.
- Provide a more personalized and customizable experience.
- Connect emotions with gift selection.
- Create a surprise and memory-building experience after purchase.
- Design and test an interactive prototype with users.
- Provide an experience that is different from traditional gift stores.

---

## 👤 Target Audience

ARIHA is designed for people who want to express their feelings through flowers and gifts, including:

- People buying gifts for family members.
- Couples and partners.
- Friends looking for meaningful gifts.
- People shopping for birthdays and special occasions.
- Users who want to send flowers without a specific occasion.
- Anyone who finds choosing the right gift difficult.

## 🧠 Core UX Concept

Traditional gift-shopping experiences usually begin with:

**Product → Browse → Choose → Buy**

ARIHA introduces a more emotional approach:

**Feeling → Occasion → Relationship → Message → Gift → Surprise → Memory**

This approach makes the experience more personal and helps users find gifts that better represent what they want to communicate.

---

## ✨ Key Experience

### 1. Understand the User

ARIHA learns about:

- The occasion
- The relationship
- The user's feelings
- The type of message they want to communicate
- Their preferences

### 2. Personalized Recommendations

The application uses the collected information to guide users toward suitable flowers and gifts.

### 3. Personalization

Users can customize their gift experience through options such as:

- Gift selection
- Flower selection
- Personal messages
- Presentation preferences

### 4. Surprise Experience

The experience continues beyond the purchase by creating an additional moment of surprise and emotional connection.

### 5. Memory

ARIHA aims to transform a simple purchase into a meaningful memory.
